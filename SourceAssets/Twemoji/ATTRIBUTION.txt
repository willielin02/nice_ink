Usage in Nice Ink: the 16 SVGs in this folder are the source artwork for the
dream-trace minigame's one-stroke motif routes (SPEC v4.0a). They are converted
to polyline trace routes by Tools/AssetPrep/TraceMotifs/ (silhouette union +
interior-contour splicing + traceability gates) and baked into
Source/NiceInk/Private/DreamTraceMotifData.h. The in-game routes are derived
works of these graphics.
