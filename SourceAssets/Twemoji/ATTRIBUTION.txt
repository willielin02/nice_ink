Usage in Nice Ink (08-03 final 8-motif pool): the dream-trace minigame's
one-stroke motif routes (SPEC v4.0a) come from these vector sources —
Twemoji: fan(1faad), wave(1f30a; the two-crest figure is the wave plus a
same-source 0.6x copy), dango(1f361), snake(1f40d), turtle(1f422),
sakura(1f338), torii(26e9); Wikimedia: origami crane (fattened +0.4cm to keep
spike corridors traceable). They are converted to polyline trace routes by
Tools/AssetPrep/TraceMotifs/ (silhouette union + traceability gates; interior
contours retired 08-03) and baked into
Source/NiceInk/Private/DreamTraceMotifData.h. The in-game routes are derived
works of these graphics.
